Jot 0.7.2

Fixes Notion pages failing to create, and makes a failed Notion write recoverable.

  • Transcripts with emoji no longer break the Notion write. Jot was measuring transcript length differently from Notion, so a transcript containing emoji could be rejected with a bare "Notion: failed" and no page created. Jot now splits the transcript the way Notion counts it, so the page is created cleanly.
  • See why a Notion write failed. A failed Notion row now has a Details button that shows the exact error, instead of hiding it in a hover tooltip.
  • Retry just the Notion page. When transcription succeeded but the Notion write failed, a new Retry Notion button re-creates the page from the transcript already on disk — no need to re-transcribe the whole meeting.
